Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy covers the Flitty's Big Why website and the Flitty's Big Why Uploader, a private tool that the channel owner uses to publish our own videos.

Children

Our videos are made for children, and they are published as "made for kids" on YouTube. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not run accounts, sign-ups, comments, contests or chats for viewers, and this website has no forms, cookies or analytics.

What the Uploader accesses

The Uploader signs in only with the channel owner's own Google, YouTube and Meta accounts, using OAuth. With the owner's permission it:

  • uploads our own video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules to our own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API);
  • reads the upload status of our own videos so we can confirm they published correctly;
  • posts our own videos to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

It does not access any other person's account or data, and it does not read viewers' personal information.

How we store and protect data

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and cloud workspace, are never shared or sold, and are used only for the uploads described above. Video files are our own creative work.

Google API Services

The Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Uploader uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the Uploader's access at any time at Google Account permissions and in Meta's Business Integrations settings. On revocation, stored tokens are deleted.
